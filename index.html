Our little story

Handmade, from the heart

At Lara Flowers, every arrangement begins with a feeling — a love letter, a celebration, a quiet thank you. We listen to your story, then choose each stem to tell it.

For years our neighbours in Tripoli have trusted us with their most tender moments: first dates and fiftieth anniversaries, newborns and farewells. Nothing leaves our table that we wouldn't be proud to gift ourselves.

No two bouquets are ever the same — because no two stories are.

— with love, Lara
